# The Dynasty: build order

Each build is playable on its own and adds one layer. The order follows what each layer depends on.

| Build | Focus | What it adds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.1 ✅ | World sim | Living world, Chronicle, realms, characters, intrigue, monsters |
| 0.2 ✅ | Battle map | HAS sprites, divisions, messenger orders, morale and routs, fire volley, enemy general |
| 0.3 ✅ | World battles | World wars use the battle engine: real officers, army levels, troop tiers, terrain and season. Watch a battle or auto-resolve it; results feed the Chronicle, XP, titles, captures and deaths |
| 0.4 ✅ | Overworld map | A zoomable living map built from HAS tiles: armies marching, settlements, lairs, borders, minimap, tap anything for details |
| 0.5 ✅ | You | Pick an origin (vagabond, headman, minor lord, heir), random placement, your character and dynasty, permadeath with a named successor, ironman and casual modes |
| 0.6 ✅ | Ruling | Food, taxes, guard settings, recruiting, assigning officers to armies, delegation (steward, captain, spymaster), diplomacy: marriages, alliances, war, peace terms, wards |
| 0.7 ✅ | Agents on the map | Spies, assassins, messengers, envoys, caravans and agitators as visible travelers you can intercept; rumors spreading town to town |
| 0.8 ✅ | Dynasty depth | Title management, duels and tournaments you can watch, oaths, heirs with blended traits, history generated before turn one, end-of-run saga, "same world, new life" |
| 0.9 ✅ | Content and polish | Dwarves, Wood Elves, Orcs and Lizardmen in battle, save slots, seed sharing, mod support, balance pass, polish backlog below |

| 1.0 ✅ | Release | Full regression, credits, final docs |

## 2.0: the rework

| Build | Focus | What it adds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2.1 ✅ | Huge world | Continents and islands, territories, ports and sailing, named wilds and named monsters, caves, earthquakes, wildfires and floods that change the land, a chunked map renderer for the bigger worlds |
| 2.2 ✅ | Live map-first play | The map is the main screen; time runs at 2 minutes a month with pause only; the camera follows you; start at year 1 or dropped into a random year 30–100 |
| 2.3 ✅ | Knowledge and fog | A cartographer's map that stays drawn until nature changes it; you only know what history, locals, travel and spies tell you |
| 2.4 ✅ | Dynasty Warriors battles | 3–5 bases a side with garrisons that sleep until enemies come near, 1–8 officers, the lord's reserve (Defend, Push, Reinforce), stratagems on bases, up to 30,000 troops, Mid zoom cap and officer portraits |

## Warlord (3.x)

| Phase | Name | What it adds |
|---|---|---|
| 3.1 ✅ | Road battle maps | The new main game: quick battles on road networks with lanes, passes, bridges and fords; base layouts per side; Go-to-base orders; the old game moved to /classic |
| 3.2 | Stratagems and events | Stratagem cards drawn during battle and played with command points that refill; landslides, bridge collapses, floods, wildfires and storms that close roads and force battles onto other paths |
| 3.3 | Your army | A persistent roster of officers and troop types, recruitment, upgrades, rewards, losses and upkeep |
| 3.4 | Campaign | CPU warlords with personalities, battles that grow from about 2,000 to 30,000 troops, rivals who remember you |
| 3.5 | Polish | Balance, a first-hour guide and a full scrub |
